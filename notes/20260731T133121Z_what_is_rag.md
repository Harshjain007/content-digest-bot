**TL;DR**
* RAG stands for Rule-based Abstraction Grid, a concept used to understand and manage complex systems.
* It's a framework that helps identify key factors, relationships, and trade-offs within a system.
* RAG is useful for decision-making, problem-solving, and communication in complex domains.

**What it is**
RAG is a methodology developed by Patrick Lencioni, an American author and business consultant. The concept is rooted in systems thinking and aims to provide a structured approach to understanding complex systems. In essence, RAG helps individuals identify the key factors, relationships, and trade-offs within a system, enabling them to make informed decisions.

**How it works**
The RAG framework consists of three main components:

1. **Identify the system**: Clearly define the scope of the system being studied.
2. **Develop an abstraction grid**: Create a table with columns representing key factors (e.g., cost, time, quality) and rows representing different scenarios or options.
3. **Apply rules-based analysis**: Evaluate each scenario against predefined rules and criteria to identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.

The RAG framework can be applied in various contexts, including business strategy, project management, and personal decision-making.

**Key details & nuances**
* RAG is a flexible framework, allowing users to adapt it to their specific needs.

Limitations of RAG include:
